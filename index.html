Privacy Policy

Please read this guide to understand how information may be handled when you use Little Garden Bot.

01 Information the bot may process ⌄

Depending on the features enabled and how the bot is configured, Little Garden Bot may process these categories of information:

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and message or interaction IDs when needed to respond or operate a feature.
  • Economy and game information: currency balances, inventory, pet details, trading activity, game results, cooldowns, and progress.
  • Verification submissions: answers or details submitted through button or form verification, along with review status where configured.
  • Messages and interactions: content or selections you submit to commands, buttons, forms, cooking and drink features, hugs, tarot readings, watering sprouts, and other activities.
  • Anonymous letters: the message content and technical identifiers needed to deliver or moderate the feature, depending on its implementation.
  • Presence information: online status or activity-related presence updates only if a presence-based feature is enabled, the necessary access is available, and the bot is configured to process them.
A note about accuracy: This list describes possible categories, not a guarantee that every item is collected or stored. The bot owner should verify the live BDFD commands, variables, logs, and any connected services against this policy before relying on it.
02 How information may be used ⌄

Information may be processed to:

  • Respond to commands, buttons, forms, and other interactions.
  • Maintain balances, items, pets, trades, cooldowns, and game progress.
  • Complete or review verification requests and support server moderation workflows.
  • Deliver interactive messages, including anonymous letters, and operate community features.
  • Calculate server or member statistics, including estimated presence counts if that feature is implemented.
  • Help troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and keep the bot operating reliably.

Information should be used only for purposes connected to the bot's features and administration. The bot owner should not claim that data is never shared, logged, or retained unless the actual setup has been checked.

03 Storage and retention ⌄

Some features may need to retain information so that balances, pet inventories, verification states, or preferences continue to work between interactions. Other information may be processed temporarily to respond to a command.

Where and for how long information is stored depends on the bot's BDFD configuration, enabled variables, logs, and any additional services used by the owner. This policy does not assert that no data is stored. The owner should document actual storage locations and retention periods and remove information when it is no longer needed, where feasible.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other highly sensitive information through bot features.

04 Presence and activity statistics ⌄

If a presence-based statistic is implemented, the bot may process presence updates to estimate online-member activity for a displayed count. Availability depends on Discord's permissions, gateway intents, and the bot's configuration.

A global count may be approximate: a person can belong to multiple servers, and not every member's presence is necessarily available. The bot owner should confirm whether individual presence data is retained, whether an opt-out is offered, and whether any data is sent to or stored by a service outside Discord.

If the bot does not use presence information, this section describes a possible feature and should be updated to say so before publication.

05 Anonymous letters and user content ⌄

Anonymous letter features may show a message to its recipient without displaying the sender's identity. “Anonymous” describes what the feature displays; it is not a promise that the system cannot associate a submission with technical identifiers or logs.

Avoid including private contact details or sensitive information in letters. The owner should verify whether sender IDs, message content, or moderation logs are stored and explain those practices accurately.

06 Discord and third-party services ⌄

The bot operates through Discord and may rely on Bot Designer for Discord (BDFD) and other services selected by the owner. These services may process information as part of providing their functionality and are governed by their own policies and terms.

For details about Discord's handling of account information, see Discord's Privacy Policy. The bot owner should add any other third-party services actually used by the bot before publishing this policy.

07 Your choices and privacy questions ⌄

You can choose whether to use optional bot features. You can also leave a server where the bot is present or stop interacting with the bot, though information already retained may not be deleted automatically.

For a question or request about information associated with your use of the bot, contact the owner through the support server below. The owner should review the request and explain what can be located, corrected, or deleted based on the bot's actual storage and applicable requirements.

If presence-based features are used, the owner should state clearly whether users can opt out of that processing and how to request it. Do not assume that opting out is available unless it has been implemented.

08 Security and policy updates ⌄

The owner should use reasonable measures to protect information within their control, while recognizing that no online service can guarantee absolute security.

This policy may change if the bot's features, storage, or service providers change. The date at the top indicates when this page was last updated. Material changes should be reflected here.
